# 🍔 CraveVerse

### Next-Gen Food Discovery & Restaurant Marketplace

CraveVerse is a modern, immersive food marketplace experience designed to bring **food discovery, restaurant exploration, short-form food reels, instant ordering, live delivery experiences, and intelligent food search** into one digital ecosystem.

The project focuses heavily on **premium UI/UX, motion design, responsive layouts, immersive food media, and interactive experiences** across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## 🖼️ Overview

CraveVerse combines the familiar experience of a food delivery platform with a more immersive discovery-first interface.

Instead of simply browsing restaurant lists, users can:

- Discover restaurants
- Explore food visually
- Watch food reels
- Like and share food content
- Discover signature dishes
- Search restaurants and dishes globally
- Add dishes directly to the cart
- Explore restaurant details
- Track delivery experiences
- Interact with an animated delivery rider
- Experience responsive interfaces across devices

The overall visual direction uses a **dark premium food-tech aesthetic with warm amber/orange accents**.

## 🎥 Food Reels

CraveVerse includes an Instagram-style food discovery experience.

Users can scroll through food reels featuring dishes such as:

- 🍔 Burgers
- 🥟 Kothey Momos
- 🍕 Pizza

The reels use local MP4 media from the project's `public` directory.
